### Uninstall removes the library entry

- Successful portable EXE and ZIP uninstalls now remove their entries from the visible library and save that change immediately.
- Registered uninstallers are monitored. The entry is removed after the matching registration and any known EXE confirm removal; leftover data folders do not prevent confirmation.
- Cancellation, failure and unverified removal keep the entry. Pending checks survive restarts, and **Keep tracking** stops waiting without cancelling a running uninstaller.
- A library-save failure preserves the pending entry for a later retry.

### Correct confirmation for missing apps

Stop tracking an **App not found** entry now asks to remove the missing library entry. It no longer says installed files and backups will stay in place. Normal and pending-uninstall entries have their own confirmations too.

Existing entries left behind by older versions can be removed with **Stop tracking** after updating. Apps removed outside GROUT are still retained with Find installation and Stop tracking choices.

The full packaged regression suite passed, including visible list removal, saved-library removal, pending restart recovery, cancellation/failure, save failure and state-specific confirmations. Real third-party uninstallers were not run by the automated tests.
