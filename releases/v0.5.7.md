### A quieter app status area

- Sidebar statuses now sit on the right of each entry, right-aligned, beside the app name and repository.
- Removed routine success messages such as Installed version detected, Portable app saved from release, and Marked installed manually.
- Older saved success messages are also suppressed and cleared during reconciliation.
- The selected app's message area collapses when empty. Failures and unresolved installation/update issues remain visible.
- Version reconciliation no longer replaces an existing update issue with a generic success message merely because the version is current.

The full packaged regression suite passes, including success-message suppression and preservation of actual update issues. Existing library and settings carry over.
