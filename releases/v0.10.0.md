### Organize your library and reliably track installed releases

- Arrange apps and mods in a user-managed parent/child tree. A tracked item can contain children and still have its own update action.
- Drag to reorder siblings, move whole branches, or reparent items using the marked Inside area. Ordering survives restarts; physical install folders do not move.
- Optional parent folder defaults apply to new child installations only. Add screens distinguish the library parent from the physical install folder.
- Collapsed parents show All up to date or an update/issue count. Expanded organizers hide that summary.
- Every confirmed ZIP, portable EXE or located installer installation writes a small permanent record in GROUT AppData. The release actually applied takes precedence over stale publisher version metadata. Failed/cancelled operations do not advance the recorded version.
- Recognize added download revision suffixes such as .R17. Unmatched downloads offer a direct Choose download action with a specific explanation.
- Simplified detail actions and corrected menu/tooltip contrast. Parent removal preserves child entries; physical uninstalls are blocked when they overlap other tracked installations.

D2RLoader catalog browsing is temporarily hidden. Existing tracked mods and direct mod URLs remain supported. Nexus remains disabled in public builds.

Validated by packaged regression tests and real mouse-drag tests in an isolated sample library. GROUT remains a single EXE under 1 MB.
